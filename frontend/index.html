How the model was built

The method follows Fan et al. (2026), first developed for MgO sorbents, applied here to biomass-derived porous carbons.

  1. Clean and split

    527 published experiments (Yuan et al., 2021). One duplicate removed, then split 336 / 84 / 106 into training, validation and a locked test set.

  2. Six tree models

    Random Forest, Extra Trees, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost, each tuned with Optuna.

  3. Entropy-TOPSIS weights

    The models are ranked on several error measures at once, and better models get a larger share of the final average.

  4. SHAP explanations

    Every prediction is split into one contribution per input, starting from the model's average.
