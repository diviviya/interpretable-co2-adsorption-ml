About the Prediction System

ML Workspace

Porous carbons derived from agricultural and industrial biomass wastes offer a sustainable, cost-effective pathway for carbon capture and storage (CCS). However, experimental synthesis and high-pressure gas sorption testing are labor-intensive and resource-heavy.

This platform leverages a curated dataset of 527 experimental observations characterized by 10 physical, textural, elemental, and operating features. It bridges the gap between black-box accuracy and physical chemical interpretability.

Core Pipeline Pillars

01
6 Tuned Tree Architectures

Random Forest, Extra Trees, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost tuned via Optuna.

02
Optimal Weighted Ensembling

Validation set optimization yielding the highest generalization capacity and lowest MAE (0.522 mmol/g).

03
Game-Theoretic SHAP Interpretability

Exact marginal feature attributions reveal how each pore property and operating condition impacts uptake.
