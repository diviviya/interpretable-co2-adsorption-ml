Methodology & Engineering Architecture

Complete documentation of dataset curation, feature engineering, cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning, ensembling, and explainability methods.

1. Dataset Curation & Overview

The dataset comprises 527 verified experimental data points collected from peer-reviewed literature investigating CO₂ adsorption on porous carbon materials synthesized from biomass waste precursors (agricultural residues, forestry byproducts, and industrial bio-waste).

Parameter Category Features Included Measurement Unit Scientific Role
Textural Properties Surface Area, Total Pore Volume, Micropore Volume m²/g, cm³/g, cm³/g Quantifies physical surface exposure and internal cavity space for gas condensation.
Elemental Composition C, H, N, O, S wt% Characterizes carbon skeleton purity and heteroatom chemical affinity (especially basic N sites).
Operational Conditions Temperature, Pressure °C, bar Thermodynamic state variables governing adsorption-desorption equilibrium.
Target Variable CO₂ Uptake Capacity mmol/g Equilibrium quantity of CO₂ adsorbed per gram of porous carbon.

2. Preprocessing & Leakage Prevention

  • Stratified Data Partitioning: The 527 observations were partitioned into train, validation, and held-out test splits with fixed random seeds.
  • Median Imputation in Scikit-Learn Pipelines: To prevent data leakage, SimpleImputer(strategy='median') was fitted solely on training splits and bundled directly inside each model's pipeline.
  • No Scale Distortion: Tree-based architectures do not require feature scaling, preserving original physical units throughout all steps.

3. Hyperparameter Tuning & Cross-Validation

  • Optuna Bayesian Optimization: Hyperparameters were systematically explored across hundreds of trials to maximize validation generalization.
  • Algorithms Tuned: Random Forest, Extra Trees, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost.
  • Loss Function: Mean Squared Error (MSE) minimization on 5-fold cross-validation folds.

4. Ensemble Construction Strategy

Rather than relying on simple equal-weight averaging, the ensemble leverages validation-optimized soft voting. Non-negative weights summing to 1.0 were computed to minimize root mean squared error on the held-out validation partition:

Ensemble Prediction = 0.5464 × CatBoost + 0.2766 × LightGBM + 0.1770 × XGBoost + 0.0 × (RF, ET, GB)

This optimal weighting effectively leverages CatBoost and LightGBM's superior handling of numerical features while suppressing weaker learners, outperforming all single models on the unseen test set (R² = 0.8173).

5. Engineering Limitations & Scope

While the model demonstrates high empirical accuracy, users should keep in mind the following practical boundaries:

  1. Domain Extrapolation: Predictions for surface areas exceeding 3,500 m²/g or temperatures above 150 °C extrapolate beyond the domain of the training dataset.
  2. Moisture & Competitive Sorption: The models assume pure CO₂ gas adsorption and do not currently model multi-component flue gas competitive adsorption (e.g. H₂O vapor or N₂).
  3. Kinetic Limitations: Predictions represent static thermodynamic equilibrium capacity (mmol/g) and do not model dynamic breakthrough curves or mass-transfer kinetics.
